4. PLC Control Logic (Obj. 3)
Developed in EcoStruxure Machine Expert (IEC 61131-3). Key programs:

PID Pressure Control: Maintains header pressure by modulating VFD speed. Uses derivative on measurement (not error) to avoid output spikes on setpoint changes. Anti-windup and bumpless transfer included.
Process Sequencing (SFC): IDLE → PRE-CHECK → SOFT-START → RUNNING → SOFT-STOP → FAULT
Safety Interlocks: Dry-run (flow meter), over-current (VFD fault word), over-temperature (PT100 via ATV630), under-voltage (PM5300), communication loss. Emergency stop triggers the ATV630's SIL 3 Safe Torque Off (STO) per IEC 61508 — hardwired, independent of PLC scan.
Optimization Supervisor: Configures ATV630 Sleep Mode thresholds and Energy Adaptation Mode parameters based on demand profile.

7. Energy Optimization Strategy (Obj. 6) ⭐
Affinity Laws: Q ∝ N | H ∝ N² | P ∝ N³. Reducing pump speed to 80% cuts power to ~51% — a 49% saving vs. full speed.

System Curve Calibration (Critical): Total head H_total = H_static + H_friction. The cube law applies only to the friction component; static head (elevation lift) is constant and limits how far the pump can slow. Our target application is friction-dominated (~80% friction / ~20% static), where affinity-law savings are largely realized (40–50%). For static-dominated systems, we conservatively project 25–35%.

During commissioning, the optimization engine logs operating points (flow, head, speed, power) to characterize the actual system curve and compute the achievable-vs-theoretical savings ratio. All recommendations are based on this calibrated model, not the ideal cube law alone.
