1. CORE CANON PRINCIPLE
============================================================

CANON is NOT an AI dashboard.

CANON is an engineering control loop.

The authority model is FIXED:

AI PROPOSES
      ↓
CANON MODEL DEFINES
      ↓
DETERMINISTIC VALIDATOR CHECKS
      ↓
ENGINEER APPROVES
      ↓
PLC / SIMULATOR CONTROLS
      ↓
HMI RECEIVES FEEDBACK
      ↓
CHANGE IMPACT
      ↓
REGENERATION

The Context page exists to establish:

"WHAT IS ACTUALLY KNOWN ABOUT THIS MACHINE?"

before CANON generates anything.

Therefore the Context screen must communicate:

DISCOVERED ≠ INFERRED
CONNECTED ≠ VERIFIED
VERIFIED ≠ AI GENERATED

AI MUST NEVER silently create engineering truth.
